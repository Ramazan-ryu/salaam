# Organizer Outreach Kit (P-0)

Status: Draft 0.2 · 2026-11-16 · Related: [PARTNERSHIP_STRATEGY.md](./PARTNERSHIP_STRATEGY.md), [USER_INTERVIEW_GUIDE.md](./USER_INTERVIEW_GUIDE.md#3-organizer-interview-guide-2030-min), [DATA_SOURCES_REGISTER.md](./DATA_SOURCES_REGISTER.md)

> The founder sends all messages personally. Nothing has been sent yet. Messages must **not** claim users, partners, an app or funding that don't exist. Native-speaker review of the Azerbaijani and Russian texts is recommended before first use.

## 1. Who to contact first (15 targets)

Choose from the source evaluation ([CONTENT_VALIDATION_WORKBOOK.md](./CONTENT_VALIDATION_WORKBOOK.md#3-source-evaluation-template-sourcescsv)), with a mix of: 5 small venues/galleries, 4 workshop or class hosts, 3 music venues/promoters, 3 community/student organizers. Prefer organizers who post events regularly in the pilot categories. Log each in [`p0/outreach_log.csv`](./p0/outreach_log.csv).

## 3. Follow-up (once, after 5–7 days, only if no reply)

**AZ:** Salam! Əvvəlki mesajımla bağlı qısa xatırlatma — tədbirlərinizi həftəlik siyahıya linklə əlavə etməyimə etirazınız olmazmı? Cavab verməsəniz, yenidən narahat etməyəcəyəm. Təşəkkürlər!

**RU:** Здравствуйте! Короткое напоминание о моём сообщении — не будете ли вы против, если я буду включать ваши мероприятия в еженедельную подборку со ссылкой на вас? Если ответа не будет, больше не побеспокою. Спасибо!

**EN:** Hi! A short reminder about my earlier message — would you mind if I include your events in the weekly list with a link to you? If I don't hear back, I won't bother you again. Thanks!

No reply after the follow-up → mark `no_response`, don't contact again in P-0, and **don't** include their events unless the source is otherwise permitted (see workbook permission rules).

## 4. Non-binding pilot explanation

Send only to organizers who show interest.

| Topic | What it means |
|---|---|
| What Salaam is now | An early-stage project by one founder. No app, users or partners yet. |
| What the pilot is | A free weekly list of Baku events in a channel, to learn whether people find it useful |
| What we do with your information | List event facts (title, date, time, place, price if you state it) in our own words, with a link to your page. No copying of text or images without permission. |
| What you can do | Send events via the form, or tell us which page to check. Correct or remove anything at any time; removal requests are handled within 72 hours. |
| What you don't do | Pay anything, sign anything, promise anything, or give exclusivity |
| Personal data | We don't collect your visitors' data. We keep your contact details only to talk about the pilot and delete them on request. |
| What you might get | Visibility to people looking for things to do; early input into a tool for organizers |
| Ending | Either side can stop at any time with one message |

## 5. Questions for organizers

See [USER_INTERVIEW_GUIDE.md](./USER_INTERVIEW_GUIDE.md#3-organizer-interview-guide-2030-min), section 3. In short: where they announce first, time spent, calendar formats, languages, what Salaam must never do, form vs pickup preference, how often details change, worries.

## 6. Simple submission form (fields)

Event title · organizer name · date · start time (or "not fixed yet") · place name & address · price (or "free" / "not decided") · ticket or info link · language(s) of the event · short description (their words, which they permit us to use) · contact for questions · checkbox: "I confirm I may share this information and allow Salaam to list it with a link to my page." No image upload in P-0.

## 7. Do / don't

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Say clearly it's an early experiment by one person | Claim users, partners, investors or an existing app |
| Ask permission; record the answer | Copy posts, images or texts |
| Link back to their page | Promise traffic, sales or features |
| Correct errors quickly and say thanks | Message repeatedly (max 1 follow-up) |
| Log every contact and outcome | Treat "interesting!" as commitment; only submissions count (VM-31) |
