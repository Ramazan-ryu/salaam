# User & Organizer Interview Guide (P-0)

Status: Draft 0.2 · 2026-11-16 · Related: [P0_VALIDATION_PLAN.md](./P0_VALIDATION_PLAN.md), [VALIDATION_METRICS.md](./VALIDATION_METRICS.md), [ORGANIZER_OUTREACH.md](./ORGANIZER_OUTREACH.md)

> Interviews are conducted by the founder in the participant's language (az, ru or en). Azerbaijani and Russian lines below should get a quick native-speaker review before first use. No interviews have been run yet.

**Ground rules:** ask about **past behaviour**, not future intentions. Never pitch before the behaviour section is finished. Don't say what you hope to hear. Silence is fine. "Can you show me?" beats "would you?".

## 1. Recruitment and screening

### 1.1 Who to recruit (user interviews)

| Criterion | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Age | 18–30 (target segment, D-04). Plus **2–3 comparison interviews** from another segment (expat 25–40, or a parent) to test the audience assumption |
| Location | Lives, studies or works in Baku for ≥6 months (or expat ≥3 months) |
| Activity | Went to at least one leisure activity outside home/work in the last 2 months, **or** says they wanted to but didn't (both are useful) |
| Mix | Students and working people; az-, ru- and en-preferring speakers; gender balance |
| Exclude | People working in event promotion/ticketing (they go to the organizer guide); more than 3 close friends/family in total (note closeness in the log) |

### 1.2 Recruitment message (adapt; post only where group admins allow)

**AZ:** Salam! Bakıda insanların asudə vaxt üçün tədbir və yerləri necə tapdığını öyrənirəm. 18–30 yaş arası, Bakıda yaşayan/oxuyan/işləyən biri kimi 30–40 dəqiqəlik söhbətə vaxtınız olarsa, çox minnətdar olaram. Heç nə satmıram — sadəcə təcrübənizi öyrənmək istəyirəm.

**RU:** Здравствуйте! Я изучаю, как люди в Баку находят мероприятия и места для досуга. Если вам 18–30 лет, вы живёте, учитесь или работаете в Баку и готовы уделить 30–40 минут на разговор, буду очень благодарен(на). Я ничего не продаю — мне важен ваш опыт.

**EN:** Hi! I'm researching how people in Baku find things to do in their free time. If you're 18–30, live/study/work in Baku and could spare 30–40 minutes for a conversation, I'd be very grateful. I'm not selling anything — I want to learn from your experience.

### 1.3 Screener (3 questions, by message)
1. Age range and how long you've been in Baku?
2. When did you last go out to an event, exhibition, concert, workshop or similar? (or: was there something you wanted to go to but didn't?)
3. Which language do you prefer for everyday information: Azerbaijani, Russian, English?

## 2. User interview guide (≈30–40 min + 12 min prototype)

### 2.1 Opening and consent (2 min)
> "Thank you. I'm working on an early idea about finding things to do in Baku. There are no right or wrong answers, and I'm interested in what you actually do, not what you think I want to hear. I'll take notes without your name. May I take notes? (May I record audio? It's only for my notes and is deleted within 30 days.) You can stop at any time."

AZ: "Təşəkkür edirəm. Bakıda nə ilə məşğul olmaq barədə ilkin bir ideya üzərində işləyirəm. Düzgün və ya səhv cavab yoxdur — mənə həqiqətən nə etdiyiniz maraqlıdır. Adınızı qeyd etmədən qeydlər aparacağam. İcazə verirsiniz? İstədiyiniz an dayandıra bilərsiniz."

RU: "Спасибо. Я работаю над ранней идеей о том, как находить, чем заняться в Баку. Правильных и неправильных ответов нет — мне интересно, что вы делаете на самом деле. Я буду делать заметки без вашего имени. Можно? Вы можете остановиться в любой момент."

### 2.2 Warm-up (3 min)
1. What do you usually do on a free evening? And on weekends?

### 2.3 Last time (core behaviour, 10 min)
2. Tell me about the **last time** you went to an event or new place in your free time. How did you first hear about it?
3. What did you do between hearing about it and going? (checked times, asked friends, bought tickets…)
4. Who did you go with? How did you agree on it?
5. Was anything about the information wrong, unclear or hard to find?

### 2.4 Current sources (5 min)
6. Where do you look when you want to find something to do? *(If comfortable)* Could you show me on your phone which apps/accounts/channels you use?
7. Roughly how many accounts or channels do you check? Do you follow any Telegram channels or Instagram pages for this?
8. Which language are these sources usually in? Is that a problem for you?

### 2.5 Problems (5 min)
9. In the **last month**, was there something you would have liked to attend but found out too late or missed? What happened? *(code VM-21 only if a specific example)*
10. Has information ever turned out to be wrong — cancelled, sold out, different time? Tell me about the last time. *(VM-23)*
11. How do you know whether information is still current?

### 2.6 Notifications and planning (3 min)
12. Which apps are you happy to get notifications from? Which have you turned off, and why?
13. When do you usually decide what to do on a weekend — earlier in the week or same day?

### 2.7 Concept reaction (neutral, 3 min) — only after 2.3–2.6
14. "Some people imagine one place that lists what's on in Baku today, checked for accuracy, in your language, with a personal character that suggests things. What would you expect it to do? What would make you stop using it?"
*(Do not ask "Would you use it?" — record unprompted reactions only.)*

### 2.8 Prototype test (≈12 min)
Use the static prototypes from the **"Salaam — First Look: Home Concepts A & C"** design canvas (Concept A / Concept C), plus linked frames for the list and detail. Protocol in [P0_VALIDATION_PLAN.md](./P0_VALIDATION_PLAN.md#5-avatar-validation-f): 5-second impression → T1 find tonight → T2 save → T3 weekend → (T4 change outfit, A only) → SEQ → closing questions. Counterbalance A/C order.

### 2.9 Close (2 min)
15. "Is there anything I should have asked?"
16. Offer (no pressure): "I'm testing a free weekly list of Baku events in a channel. Would you like the link?" and "Would you like to be on a list to test an app later?" Record yes/no only.

## 3. Organizer interview guide (≈20–30 min)

Use with venues, galleries, promoters, workshop hosts, student clubs. Consent opening as in 2.1.

1. Tell me about your last event. How did you announce it? Where did you post **first**?
2. How long does announcing an event take you each time? Which part is most annoying?
3. Where do most of your visitors say they heard about you?
4. Do you publish a calendar anywhere (website, ticketing site, Telegram)? Is it in a format others can use (e.g. calendar file, feed)?
5. In which languages do you announce? Do you get foreign visitors?
6. If a free service listed your events with a link to your page, what would you want it to show, and what should it **never** do? (images, text, prices)
7. Would you prefer to send events yourself (short form) or have them picked up from your own channel with your permission?
8. How often do your event details change after announcing (time, cancellation, sold out)? How do you tell people?
9. What would worry you about a service like this?
10. Close: explain the non-binding pilot ([ORGANIZER_OUTREACH.md](./ORGANIZER_OUTREACH.md#4-non-binding-pilot-explanation)) only if they are interested; never pressure.

## 5. Synthesis template

Fill after every 3–4 interviews, then finally at week 7.

```
1. Participants: count by segment, language, closeness
2. Metric tallies: VM-21 __/__ · VM-22 __/__ · VM-23 __/__ · VM-24 __/__
3. Top behaviours (how people actually discover) — with counts
4. Top problems — with counts and 1–2 anonymised quotes each
5. Sources & channels named (feeds DATA_SOURCES_REGISTER and pilot channel choice)
6. Language findings
7. Avatar test: VM-40..45 table + themes (helps / distracts / indifferent)

9. Contradictions and open questions

```

## 6. Coding guide for metrics

| Metric | Count as YES only if… | Not enough |
|---|---|---|
| VM-21 missed event | Participant gives a **specific** event/occasion in the last 30 days they missed or almost missed due to finding out late/not at all | "Yes, that happens sometimes" |
| VM-22 ≥3 sources | They name or show ≥3 distinct sources they used in the last month | Listing sources they "could" use |
| VM-23 wrong info | A **specific** recent case of cancelled/wrong time/sold-out/outdated info | General distrust |
| VM-24 pilot use | Joined after the interview without pressure **and** reports viewing ≥2 posts the following week (only if they agree to say) | Saying they will join |
| VM-44 distracting | Describes the avatar as getting in the way, slowing them, annoying, childish | Neutral or "cute" |
